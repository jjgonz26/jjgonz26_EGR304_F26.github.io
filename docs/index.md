## Introduction

* This is a breakdown of our teams sensing bowl. The primary function of this board is to monitor the amount of food is in the bowl and to communicate to the other boards with a digital signal when to release more food into the bowl and when to stop. 

### Project Summary

* Our team is trying to offer a reliable solution for pet owners to feed their furry friends when they are away. Our goal is to create an autonomous system that is able to know when the bowl of food needs to be refilled and can fill up in the absence of the owner ensuring that their pet have access to food throughout the day. For more context on the team's development process see to the link below to have access to the teams report which will also have links to the other team member's websites for a deeper look into all of the boards.
