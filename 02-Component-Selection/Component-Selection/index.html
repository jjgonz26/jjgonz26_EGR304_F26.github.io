    Component Selection

    Table 1: Op Amp

    Solution Pros Cons

    * Option 1.
    * MCP6004-I/P Quad Operational Amplifier
    * $0.60/each
    *Link to product    		 * Operates on 5V
    * Four amplifiers in one
    * Low supply current (100 μA per amplifier)
    * Rail-to-rail inputs and outputs    		 * Maximum Supply Voltage 6V needs regulation from 9V power source
    * Lower bandwidth (1 MHz) than the TL974IN (12 MHz)
    *Input offset voltage can introduce small measurements errors

    * Option 2.
    * TL974IN Quad Operational Amplifier
    * $1.66/each
    * Link to product    		 * Works with 5V supply
    * Four amplifiers in one
    * Higher bandwidth(12 MHz)    		 * More expensive
    * Higher power consumption
    *Limited input common mode voltage range

    * Option 3.
    * LM324N Quad Operational Amplifier
    * $0.58/each
    * Link to product    		 * Operates on 5V
    * Four amplifiers in one
    * Inexpensive    		 * Not rail-to-rail
    * Input common-mode range does not extend to the positive 5V supply rail
    *Higher input bias current (45nA) than the MCP6004 (1pA)

    Choice: Option 1: MCP6004-I/P Quad Operational Amplifier

    Table 2: Light Sensor

    Solution Pros Cons

    * Option 1.
    * PDV-P8103 CdS Photoresistor
    * $0.72/each
    *Link to product    		 * Inexpensive
    * High dark resistance (500 kOhms minimum)
    * Resistance changes with light exposure    		 * Requires a voltage-divider circuit
    * Lower minimum dark resistance than option 2
    *Ambient Lighting may affect readings

    * Option 2.
    * NSL-A95013 CdS Photoresistor
    * $0.88/each
    * Link to product    		 * High minimum dark resistance (1.2 MOhms)
    * Resistance changes with light exposure
    * Good at detecting blocked light    		 * More expensive than Option 1
    * requires a voltage divider circuit
    *Ambient lighting may affect readings

    * Option 3.
    * Adafruit 161 CdS Photoresistor
    * $0.95/each
    * Link to product    		 * Simple design
    * Through hole installation
    * Detects changes in light when sensor is blocked    		 * Most Expensive
    * Low Minimum dark resistance (200 kOhms)
    *Light resistance is not listed

    Table 3: Voltage Regulator

    Solution Pros Cons

    * Option 1.
    * NJM7805FA 5V Linear Voltage Regulator
    * $1.85/each
    *Link to product    		 * Fixed 5V Output
    * Supports up to 1.5A
    * Through Hole Mounting    		 * Most expensive
    *Lower maximum operating temperature (85 °C)
    *Less energy efficient than a switching regulator

    * Option 2.
    * MC7805ACTG 5V Linear Voltage Regultor
    * $1.07/each
    * Link to product    		 * Fixed 5V output
    * Built-in overtemperature and short-circuit protection
    * Operating temperature up to 125°C    		 *Lower rated output current (1A)
    * Produces heat when reducing 9V to 5V
    *Requires external capacitors for recommended operation

    * Option 3.
    * L7805ABV-DG 5V Linear Voltage Regulator
    * $1.53/each
    * Link to product    		 * Fixed 5V output
    * Supports up to 1.5A
    * Overtemperature and short-circuit protection    		 * More expensive than option 2
    * Produces heat when reducing 9V to 5V
    *Requires external capacitors for recommended operation
